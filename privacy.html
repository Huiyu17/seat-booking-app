Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. What We Collect

This app stores seat booking data, language preference, and theme preference locally on your device using localStorage. No personal data is transmitted to any server.

2. Why We Collect It

To remember your bookings and display preferences between sessions.

3. How It Is Used

Data is used solely to provide app functionality. It is never shared with third parties.

4. Cookie & Storage Choices

You may clear stored data at any time via your browser settings or developer tools (Application → Local Storage).

5. Contact

For questions, contact the project team.
